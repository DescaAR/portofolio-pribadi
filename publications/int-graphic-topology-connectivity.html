Connectivity of Int-Graphic Topology on Directed Graphs

Desca Affajry Rais · YD Sumanto · Solikhin
DRAFT
Year2026

Summary

This manuscript characterizes connectivity of the int-graphic topology generated by in-neighborhoods of a finite simple directed graph. It connects topological connectivity with a minimal-open-neighborhood intersection graph and with the preorder induced by inclusion of out-neighborhoods.

Keywords

Alexandroff spacedirected graphint-graphic topology

Research Question

Which graph-theoretic structures characterize connectedness of the int-graphic topology associated with a finite directed graph?

Methodology

The study uses finite Alexandroff-space structure, minimal open neighborhoods, the Minimal Open Neighborhood Intersection Graph $\Gamma(X,\tau)$, and the preorder induced by out-neighborhood inclusion.

Main Results

Topological–graph equivalence
A finite Alexandroff space $(X,\tau)$ is connected if and only if its Minimal Open Neighborhood Intersection Graph $\Gamma(X,\tau)$ is connected.
Preorder characterization
For int-graphic topology, connectivity can be described through finite chains whose consecutive vertices admit a common upper bound under the out-neighborhood inclusion preorder.
Connected components
The induced chain relation is an equivalence relation, and its equivalence classes are exactly the vertex sets of the connected components.
