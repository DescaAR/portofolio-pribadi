Connectivity of Int-Graphic Topology on Directed Graphs

Desca Affajry Rais · YD Sumanto · Solikhin
DRAFT
Year2026
DOI—
StatusDraft manuscript

Summary

This manuscript characterises connectivity in the int-graphic topology induced by the in-neighbourhoods of a finite simple directed graph. It relates topological connectivity to the Minimal Open Neighborhood Intersection Graph and to the preorder determined by out-neighbourhood inclusion.

Keywords

Alexandroff spacedirected graphint-graphic topology

Research Question

Which graph-theoretic structures characterise connectivity in the int-graphic topology associated with a finite directed graph?

Methodology

The analysis draws on finite Alexandroff spaces, minimal open neighbourhoods, the Minimal Open Neighborhood Intersection Graph $\Gamma(X,\tau)$, and the preorder induced by out-neighbourhood inclusion.

Main Results

Topological–graph equivalence
A finite Alexandroff space $(X,\tau)$ is connected if and only if its Minimal Open Neighborhood Intersection Graph $\Gamma(X,\tau)$ is connected.
Preorder characterization
In the int-graphic topology, connectivity can be characterised by finite chains in which successive vertices admit a common upper bound under the out-neighbourhood inclusion preorder.
Connected components
The resulting chain relation is an equivalence relation whose classes coincide precisely with the vertex sets of the connected components.
