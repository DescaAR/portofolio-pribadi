Modular Total Edge Irregular Labeling of Corona Product of Cycle $C_4$ with Path and Null Graphs

Desca Affajry Rais · Niswah Qonita · Robertus Heri Soelistyo Utomo
PRESENTED
Year2026
DOI—
StatusOral conference presentation

Summary

This work determines the modular total edge irregularity strength of the corona graphs $C_4 \odot \overline{K}_n$ and $C_4 \odot P_n$. Explicit labeling constructions are developed and proved to attain the exact values for every $n\ge1$.

Keywords

modular total edge irregular labelingcorona productpathcyclenull graph

Research Question

What are the exact modular total edge irregularity strengths of $C_4 \odot \overline{K}_n$ and $C_4 \odot P_n$?

Methodology

The proof constructs explicit total labelings. For $C_4 \odot \overline{K}_n$, the construction is organized by congruence classes of $n$ modulo $3$; for $C_4 \odot P_n$, a closed-form construction is developed for $n\ge2$ after treating the base case separately.

Main Results

Null-graph corona
$\operatorname{mtes}(C_4 \odot \overline{K}_n)=\left\lceil\frac{4n+6}{3}\right\rceil$ for every $n\ge1$.
Path corona
$\operatorname{mtes}(C_4 \odot P_n)=\left\lceil\frac{8n+2}{3}\right\rceil$ for every $n\ge1$.
Optimality
The edge weights form consecutive integer sequences whose residues modulo the number of edges yield bijections, establishing optimality of the constructions.
