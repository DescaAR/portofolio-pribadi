Distance Spectra of Corona Graphs of Complete Graphs and Paths via Invariant Subspaces

Desca Affajry Rais · Suryoto, S.Si., M.Si.
DRAFT

Summary

This manuscript investigates the distance spectrum of the corona graph $K_n \odot P_m$ when the attached path is nonregular. By exploiting the block structure of the distance matrix and decomposing its invariant subspaces, the analysis reduces the characteristic-polynomial problem from order $n(m+1)$ to matrices of order $m+1$.

Keywords

distance spectrumcorona graphblock matrixinvariant subspaceKronecker product

Research Question

How can the distance spectrum of $K_n \odot P_m$ be characterised for nonregular $P_m$, where the conventional regularity-based corona formulas do not directly apply?

Methodology

Vertices are indexed according to corresponding positions in the $n$ copies of $P_m$. The resulting distance matrix admits the representation $D=A\otimes J_n+B\otimes I_n$ and is subsequently decomposed using $\mathbb{R}^n=\mathbf{1}_n^{\perp}\oplus \operatorname{span}\{\mathbf{1}_n\}$.

Main Results

Invariant-subspace reduction
The matrix $D$ is orthogonally similar to $n-1$ copies of $M_0=B$ together with one copy of $M_n=nA+B$.
Characteristic-polynomial factorization
The characteristic polynomial consequently factors into the contributions of two reduced matrices, replacing a single large-scale spectral computation with two problems of order $m+1$.
Verification cases
An explicit spectrum is obtained for $K_n \odot P_2$, while cases such as $K_3 \odot P_3$, $K_3 \odot P_4$, and $K_4 \odot P_3$ demonstrate the reduction for nonregular paths.
