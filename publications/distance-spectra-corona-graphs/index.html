Distance Spectra of Corona Graphs of Complete Graphs and Paths via Invariant Subspaces

Summary

This manuscript studies the distance spectrum of the corona graph $K_n \odot P_m$ when the attached path is nonregular. Its central reduction expresses the full distance matrix through block structure and invariant subspaces, reducing the characteristic-polynomial problem from order $n(m+1)$ to matrices of order $m+1$.

Keywords

distance spectrumcorona graphblock matrixinvariant subspaceKronecker product

Research Question

How can the distance spectrum of $K_n \odot P_m$ be characterized when $P_m$ is nonregular and the standard regularity-based corona formulas are no longer directly applicable?

Methodology

Vertices are grouped by corresponding positions in the $n$ copies of $P_m$. The distance matrix is written in the form $D=A\otimes J_n+B\otimes I_n$, then decomposed using $\mathbb{R}^n=\mathbf{1}_n^{\perp}\oplus \operatorname{span}\{\mathbf{1}_n\}$.

Main Results

Invariant-subspace reduction
The matrix $D$ is orthogonally similar to $n-1$ copies of $M_0=B$ together with one copy of $M_n=nA+B$.
Characteristic-polynomial factorization
The characteristic polynomial factors into contributions from the two reduced matrices, replacing one large spectral problem by two problems of order $m+1$.
Verification cases
An explicit spectrum is obtained for $K_n \odot P_2$, while cases such as $K_3 \odot P_3$, $K_3 \odot P_4$, and $K_4 \odot P_3$ illustrate the reduction for nonregular paths.
