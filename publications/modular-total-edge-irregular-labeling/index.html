Modular Total Edge Irregular Labeling of Corona Product of Cycle $C_4$ with Path and Null Graphs

Desca Affajry Rais · Niswah Qonita · Robertus Heri Soelistyo Utomo
PRESENTED
Year2026
DOI—
StatusOral conference presentation

Summary

This work establishes the exact modular total edge irregularity strength of the corona graphs $C_4 \odot \overline{K}_n$ and $C_4 \odot P_n$. Explicit labelling constructions are provided and shown to attain these values for every $n\ge1$.

Keywords

modular total edge irregular labelingcorona productpathcyclenull graph

Research Question

What are the exact modular total edge irregularity strengths of $C_4 \odot \overline{K}_n$ and $C_4 \odot P_n$?

Methodology

The proof proceeds by constructing explicit total labellings. For $C_4 \odot \overline{K}_n$, the construction is organised according to congruence classes of $n$ modulo $3$; for $C_4 \odot P_n$, a closed-form construction is obtained for $n\ge2$ following a separate treatment of the base case.

Main Results

Null-graph corona
$\operatorname{mtes}(C_4 \odot \overline{K}_n)=\left\lceil\frac{4n+6}{3}\right\rceil$ for every $n\ge1$.
Path corona
$\operatorname{mtes}(C_4 \odot P_n)=\left\lceil\frac{8n+2}{3}\right\rceil$ for every $n\ge1$.
Optimality
The resulting edge weights form consecutive integer sequences whose residues modulo the edge cardinality are bijective, thereby establishing the optimality of the constructions.
